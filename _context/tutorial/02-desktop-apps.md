# 02. Download the Claude and ChatGPT desktop apps

The browser versions see what you paste. The desktop apps can see folders on your computer, and later drive apps and the screen. Both install the way you've always installed software: a website, a download, an installer. That is the first of two doors; the next page opens the second one, the terminal, for everything that has no icon.

If you installed either app in week 1, skip the download for that one and just do the sign-in check.

## 2.1 Claude

**Mac**

1. Go to https://claude.com/download and click the Mac download. Pick **Apple Silicon** or **Intel** to match your machine (Apple menu → About This Mac; "Apple M1/M2/M3/M4" is Apple Silicon).
2. Open the downloaded `.dmg`. Drag the Claude icon onto **Applications**.
3. Eject the dmg (the eject symbol beside it in Finder's sidebar) and open **Claude** from Applications or Spotlight. macOS asks whether you're sure about something from the internet; you are.

**Windows**

1. Go to https://claude.com/download and click the Windows download. Most laptops want **x64**; a Snapdragon or other ARM laptop wants **ARM64** (Settings → System → About → System type).
2. Run the installer. SmartScreen may warn about an app from the internet; run it anyway.
3. Open **Claude** from the Start menu.

**Sign in**

4. Log in with your **Harvard email** and choose **single sign-on** when offered, same as the browser. You should see the conversations from step 1 in the sidebar. If you see an empty free account, log out and sign in again with SSO.
5. Look at the left sidebar: **Chat**, **Cowork**, **Code**. Those last two are what the desktop app has that the browser doesn't. Step 8 uses both.

## 2.2 ChatGPT

**Mac**

1. Go to https://chatgpt.com/download (or openai.com/chatgpt/download) and click the macOS download.
2. Open the `.dmg`, drag **ChatGPT** to Applications, eject, open. Same internet warning; same answer.

**Windows**

1. Open the **Microsoft Store** app, search **ChatGPT**, and check the publisher is **OpenAI**. Install.
2. Open **ChatGPT** from the Start menu.

**Sign in**

3. Log in with your **Harvard email** and **single sign-on**. Check the account menu shows the Harvard workspace.
4. Look at the top-left menu: **ChatGPT** or **Codex**. Inside ChatGPT, a toggle at the top: **Chat** / **Work**. Step 8 uses Work and Codex.

## 2.3 If something goes wrong

- **The app opens but the sign-in lands in a free account.** Wrong method. Log out, log in again, and take the SSO option when it appears.
- **macOS says the app "can't be opened because it is from an unidentified developer."** Right-click the app → Open, once. Both apps are signed; this is rare.
- **Windows Store won't install.** Sign in to the Store with any Microsoft account, or install from the ChatGPT website's Windows download if one is offered. On an ARM laptop, both apps have ARM builds; take those.
- **An old Claude from week 1 is stuck.** Quit it fully (Cmd+Q on Mac, right-click the taskbar icon on Windows), open again, and let it update.
