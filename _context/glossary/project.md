# Project

**A Project is a saved workspace that keeps related chats together, and in Cowork, links Claude to an actual folder on your computer.**

## In plain terms

In a chat app's web interface, a Project is mostly an organizer: you can drop a conversation into one to keep a body of work together rather than scattered across loose chats, and its standing instructions apply to everything inside it. In [Cowork](claude-cowork.md), a project becomes more than tidiness, "work in a project" links Claude to a real folder on your machine, which is what lets it read and change those files.

The reason this belongs in the context story rather than just being a folder-organization feature: choosing to keep a task in its own project, and starting a "new task" rather than continuing an unrelated thread, is a deliberate decision about what Claude has in [context](context.md) right now, keeping stray material from an earlier conversation out of view.

## Why it matters this term

It is the practical, everyday handle for [context engineering](context-engineering.md): the difference between Claude working from exactly the right folder and Claude working from whatever happened to be in the last open chat.
