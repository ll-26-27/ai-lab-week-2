# Artifact

**An artifact is a deliverable a model produces next to the conversation, a document, chart, or small interactive page, rather than buried in the chat text.**

## In plain terms

Most replies are just text in the thread. Sometimes you want something more like a finished object: a clean handout, a sortable table, a small working web page. Claude can produce these as artifacts, which open in their own panel that you can read, copy, download, or ask Claude to revise in place, rather than starting over from scratch each time.

The shift worth noticing is one of expectation: a model is not only something you ask, it is something that can make things for you. A messy spreadsheet can come back as a sortable page; rough notes as a formatted handout. An artifact is still built entirely from [context](context.md), so the same rule applies as everywhere else: give it the real material and a clear sense of the audience and format, not a vague gesture at what you want. Many artifacts are written in [Markdown](markdown.md) or as small [HTML](html.md) pages, and both are visible and editable if you want to see how they were built.

## Why it matters this term

It widens what you think to ask for: not just an answer, but a real deliverable you or a classmate can open, share, and reuse.
