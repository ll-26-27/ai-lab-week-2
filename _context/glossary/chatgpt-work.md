# ChatGPT Work

**Work is ChatGPT as an agent: you describe an outcome, and it plans and produces a document, spreadsheet, presentation, or Site, asking you to approve the important steps.**

## In plain terms

The ChatGPT desktop app holds Chat and Work under one toggle. Chat is the familiar back-and-forth conversation. Work is the more ambitious mode, closer to [Claude Cowork](claude-cowork.md) than to plain chat: it can open a local folder or a local project and, with your permission, read and change files there and use other desktop apps, not just talk about the task. Whether it can reach your local files at all depends on your plan and your workspace's settings; it is still rolling out broadly.

Work also runs skills the same way Codex does, `@skill-name` in the prompt, from the same `.agents/skills` folders, and it can run scheduled or event-triggered tasks, once, on a timer, or when something happens in a connected app like Slack. Its messages and task context may be stored in the cloud even when the actual work happens on your machine, which is worth knowing before you point it at anything sensitive.

## Why it matters this term

It is ChatGPT's answer to the "agent that can touch my folder" idea this course keeps returning to, and comparing it against Cowork and Codex on the same task is one of the week's built-in exercises.
