# Data sensitivity

**Data sensitivity is a plain question worth asking before you paste, upload, or connect anything to a model: how sensitive is this material, and does it belong here?**

## In plain terms

Not everything you might hand a model is equally low-stakes. A public reading is fine anywhere; a classmate's private draft, unpublished research data, or anything with a real person's grades, health, or identifying details attached is not something to paste into a chat, drag into [Cowork](claude-cowork.md), or leave sitting in a folder connected to an agent without thinking about it first. Harvard, like most universities, has its own tiers for exactly this reason, and the practical version for this course is simple: use only material you would be comfortable having read by whoever operates the tool, and treat anything more sensitive with real caution until you know the specific tool's terms.

This intersects directly with two features you will use this term: [memory](memory.md), where a model saves notes about your work across sessions, and Cowork or a connected folder, where a model reads files directly rather than only what you paste. Both are exactly where sensitive material can accumulate without anyone deciding it should.

## Why it matters this term

It is the standing caution behind every "point this at a folder" or "paste this in" instruction in the tutorial: convenient does not mean automatically appropriate for whatever you happen to be working on.
