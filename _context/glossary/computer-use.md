# Computer use

**Computer use is a model taking screenshots of your screen, deciding where to click, clicking, typing, and looking again, in any app rather than only a browser or a folder.**

## In plain terms

[Claude in Chrome](claude-in-chrome.md) sees and drives one browser; computer use sees and drives the whole screen, which is the last rung on this course's ladder of "how much reach does this agent have." Both Claude and ChatGPT offer a version of it in their desktop apps, gated by plan and platform, so it may or may not appear depending on your account. In Claude it needs two macOS permissions to be granted first, Accessibility, so it can click and type, and Screen Recording, so it can see the screen, both toggled from Settings.

Once on, it behaves the way any careful agent should: it asks before taking over the full screen the first time, it asks per app before using one, and some categories, like banking and crypto exchanges, are blocked outright regardless of what you allow. Close anything on screen you would not want photographed, email, banking, a friend's messages, before starting a session.

## Why it matters this term

It is the clearest demonstration in this course that "what an agent can do" is "what tools its harness grants it": the model is unchanged, the reach is what grows from a folder to a browser to the whole desktop.
